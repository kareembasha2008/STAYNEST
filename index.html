CAMPUS CODE OF CONDUCT

Hostel Rules & Living Policies

To ensure safety, academic focus, and mutual respect among all residents, all STAYNEST members adhere to our transparent living guidelines.

01

Entry, Exit & Curfew Timings

  • Standard Curfew: The main residence biometric turnstiles lock at 10:00 PM on weekdays and 10:30 PM on Saturdays/Sundays.
  • Late Pass Approval: Students with legitimate college lab projects, library hours, or coaching must submit an online e-gate pass via the warden portal prior to 7:00 PM.
  • Biometric Attendance: Mandatory nightly thumbprint check-in between 9:30 PM and 10:00 PM ensures every student is accounted for and safe.
  • Night-Out Passes: Overnight stays outside the hostel require written parental confirmation via verified SMS or email to the warden desk.
02

Room Cleanliness & Waste Disposal

  • Daily Housekeeping Access: Residents must grant access to our verified housekeeping staff between 10:00 AM and 2:00 PM for daily vacuuming and bathroom scrubbing.
  • Waste Segregation: Wet organic waste and dry plastic/paper waste must be placed in their respective segregated corridor bins.
  • Personal Room Cooking: Cooking using electric stoves, hotplates, or LPG cylinders inside residential bedrooms is strictly prohibited to prevent fire hazards.
  • Sanitary Hygiene: Please utilize dedicated sanitary disposal bins in attached washrooms; flushing foreign objects is penalized.
03

Visitor & Parent Policies

  • Parent Visiting Lounge: Parents, legal guardians, and siblings are warmly welcome in our ground floor Visitor Café & Lounge between 10:00 AM and 7:00 PM.
  • Dormitory Privacy: Male visitors (including fathers) are not permitted into Girls Wings, and female visitors are not permitted into Boys residential corridors beyond the main lobby.
  • Overnight Guests: Non-resident friends and outside guests are not allowed overnight stays in student rooms. Subsidized guest rooms can be reserved in advance for visiting parents.
  • Visitor ID Registration: All guests must provide valid government photo identification (Aadhaar / Voter ID) at the security reception desk.
04

Noise Restrictions & Quiet Hours

  • Mandatory Quiet Hours: Strict silence is enforced in all residential corridors, bedrooms, and reading zones from 10:30 PM to 06:30 AM every day.
  • Headphones Policy: Watching videos, listening to music, or attending online video calls inside shared rooms must be conducted with earphones/headphones.
  • 24/7 Study Hall: Students wishing to participate in collaborative group discussions or late-night exam prep are encouraged to utilize our sound-isolated Discussion Rooms.
05

Property Safety & Electrical Usage

  • Approved Appliances: Laptops, mobile chargers, study lamps, tablets, and low-power trimmers are permitted. High-draw appliances (immersion water coils, electric room heaters) are prohibited without prior approval.
  • Switch-Off Discipline: Lights, fans, and air conditioners must be switched off when exiting bedrooms. Conserving energy is our shared community responsibility.
  • Property Damage: Any deliberate defacement of walls, furniture, or smart electronics will be billed to the resident's security deposit at actual replacement cost.
06

Zero-Tolerance Substance & Anti-Ragging Policy

  • 100% Smoke, Alcohol & Drug-Free: STAYNEST maintains a zero-tolerance stance against tobacco, vape/e-cigarettes, alcoholic beverages, and illicit substances on campus.
  • Strict Anti-Ragging Mandate: In compliance with Supreme Court and UGC directives, any intimidation, ragging, or harassment leads to immediate police referral and immediate hostel expulsion.
  • Inclusive Atmosphere: Mutual respect across religion, state, caste, and gender identity is mandatory. Harassment of any employee or fellow resident is treated with strict disciplinary action.
